# Playable prologue contract — future proposal

**Unimplemented; outside M014.** Target a 15–25-minute opening in five short
chapters. Time estimates describe a first playthrough, not real-time gates.
Follow the [narrative bible](GOON_NARRATIVE_DESIGN_BIBLE.md) and
[current runtime](GOON_WORLD_RUNTIME.md); do not invent a second simulation.
The [roadmap](GOON_MILESTONE_ROADMAP.md) reserves M014 for navigation.

| Chapter | Player choice and actual-system integration required later | Persistent consequence / alternative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. A Place to Sleep | Select a starting background/contact and accept disclosed room terms. Inspect p031, local services and the actual TU/action allowances. Start from the real $40 unless a separately approved background contract changes it. | Record background, room entitlement and one chosen obligation. A housing refusal needs another valid shelter/support start, with no invented rent debt. |
| 2. An Old Acquaintance | Meet one original ordinary coworker through an eligible, authored contact; discuss an owed pickup or shared shift. Use a future directed relationship/commitment extension through commands. | Keep a reference, decline a favor or renegotiate. Refusal can lose one offer while preserving lawful work. No guaranteed famous villain or universal loyalty. |
| 3. The First Offer | Compare a lawful repair/service shift with an existing-style criminal delivery offer. Show funded terms, three-action cost, actual route/return TU and known exposure before acceptance. | One accepted offer/reservation with stable ID; declined/expired opportunities remain recorded. The lawful route must be genuinely completable. Current courier is faction work, so it cannot be relabeled lawful employment without a new contract. |
| 4. Something Goes Wrong | An authored conflicting obligation, misrepresented delivery or signaled route disruption demands a choice: honor a promise, negotiate, disclose, abandon or accept responsibility. Apply real route and job/legal rules only to acts actually performed. | Bounded lost pay/access, a kept/broken commitment or a documented legal consequence. No phantom crime, mandatory combat or endless restart. Use the actual legal source adapter if a non-job incident requires a case. |
| 5. Tomorrow Is Another Day | Review what changed, choose tomorrow's commitment, explicitly advance once, then save. Reopening the recap cannot settle again. | Starting-history record references real command/journey/contract IDs and known relationship changes. Freedom, lawful work, independent crime and continuing support remain viable. |

The background is a transferable capability/contact/obligation, not an immutable
criminal class. Original draft workers may be selected only after their authored
content and relationship integration are approved. Do not copy a published script,
video character, joke or scene. The landlord/coworker need not be famous, a full
faction actor or a guaranteed permanent contact. Dialogue offers express real
choices or are explicitly flavor; reaching a text node alone cannot grant effects.

Initial finances, home and travel use the real career state. Future lawful wages,
employment terms, obligations and NPC-specific memories require their own small
validated extensions; they do not exist merely because this contract names them.
Pin accepted terms and content versions, validate eligibility, settle once, and
record before/after values with command/domain identities. Do not reinterpret old
save histories as a completed prologue. Existing careers can skip it completely.

Before implementation, approve one background set, original contact, obligation,
funded lawful/unlawful opportunity pair, incident and bounded outcomes. Prove
both paths under zero cash, refused work, absent contacts, exhausted TU/actions,
closure, legal response and save/resume at every chapter. Compare replay across
insertion orders/fresh processes; reject invalid choices atomically. The opening
must lead to useful play without premium currency, wall-clock waiting, a villain
meeting, compulsory violence or repeating errands for XP. Summaries/skips preserve
chosen outcomes; they do not reroll eligibility or repay work.

Meaningful decisions concern promises, livelihood, risk and information; observable
feedback is actual payment/access, recorded trust and documented consequences.
Variety comes from differing obligations, credible sources and available support.
Potential grind is a fixed commute/favor chain; repeated tasks must change their
stakes. Five chapters and a dialogue menu alone remain shallow without real
contracts and persistent consequences. M014 implements none of this prologue.
