# Gotham: The Goon — Faction and Employer Design Matrix

Status: **proposal only**, M013.1. None of these employers, recruitment tests,
contracts, or personality systems is implemented or approved for implementation.
See the [bible's status legend](GOON_NARRATIVE_DESIGN_BIBLE.md#authority-and-feature-status).
This is an unofficial DC-setting design document, not an assertion of rights or
a claim that the proposed workplace rules are canonical.

## Reading the matrix

The ordinary worker evaluates an employer, not a collectible villain card.
An employer, an organization, its economic profile, and a worker's affiliation
are separate definitions. The current Harbor/Carnival/Iron keys are preserved;
none is automatically renamed or treated as a hidden DC organization.

Compensation below describes proposed contract structures, not approved prices.
Advertised pay, guaranteed pay, reliability, and personal risk must be presented
separately. A prestigious employer need not pay better. No contract requires
premium currency or an online service. Current job payouts are success rewards,
not wages debited from an employer treasury; funding those contracts would need
an explicit future economic rule and settlement tests.

Two tables form one matrix using the same employer rows. The first covers entry
and everyday conditions; the second covers advancement and persistent fallout.
All rows require refusal/exit alternatives, bounded losses, and an observable
route to discovering unreliable terms. Different cultures must change choices,
not merely change a risk multiplier or reward color.

## Entry and workplace conditions

| Proposed employer / organization | Recruitment requirements | Expected compensation | Operational risk | Loyalty expectations and leadership temperament |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Penguin — commercial/criminal network** | A reference, discretion, and a trial handling a legitimate-looking service obligation; several skill approaches. | Stated shift pay plus negotiated service commission; access to contacts can be valuable, but debt terms are disclosed. | Moderate routine exposure, severe risk when sensitive information or assets are involved. | Values usefulness, appearances, and controlled information; transactional, status-conscious, coercive when threatened. |
| **Joker — unstable criminal entourage** | A volatile invitation or an intermediary's sponsorship; willingness to abandon an unsafe trial must remain possible. | Large advertised bonuses with visibly uncertain payment; actual terms require verification. Never a mandatory progression employer. | Extreme, including danger from the employer; warning signs precede commitment. | Loyalty tests can conflict with survival; deliberately destabilizing, theatrical, cruel. Predictable loyalty farming would defeat the premise. |
| **Black Mask — coercive organized-crime outfit** | A sponsor and demonstrated reliability; intimidation is not the only proposed entry approach. | Scheduled pay with explicit deductions and promotion promises; strong leverage over workers. | High internal and external risk, including retaliation and disposability. | Demands obedience and control; calculated domination and punitive responses. Cruelty is a hazard, not shorthand for competence. |
| **Two-Face — procedurally theatrical organization** | A witnessed agreement and a test of following a declared rule under competing obligations. | Contracted pay with a clearly announced chance-dependent bonus or assignment clause. | High: a rule can be consistent while its stakes remain dangerous. | Demands adherence to his process; legalistic preparation coexists with chance-driven decisions. Do not turn real mental illness into a gameplay diagnosis. |
| **Riddler — specialist operation cell** | A new, accessible problem-solving test with an alternate practical approach or sourced hint. | Completion fee plus credit for verifiable technical work; reputation may be claimed by management. | Moderate physical risk on support work, high risk when civilians or theatrical traps are involved. | Wants proof and recognition; ego and the need to demonstrate superiority can override practical success. |
| **Mr. Freeze — proposed technical support cell** | Demonstrated care with sensitive equipment, reliable handling, and respect for private information. | Specialist service fee or retainer with a narrow agreed deliverable; supplies require their own budget. | High environmental/specialist hazards; emergency demands can override a normal shift. | Values precision and the central personal objective; focused and emotionally remote, not automatically a benevolent employer. A standing employment cell is our proposal. |
| **Scarface / Ventriloquist — proposed intermediary-run crew** | Sponsorship, discretion, and clear confirmation of who is authorized to issue a task. | Crew share or retainer with a recorded handoff; competing instructions create payment disputes. | High command ambiguity and retaliation risk; ordinary coworkers need practical safety routes. | Loyalty is expressed through intermediaries and status rituals; threats and dominance surround a deliberately unsettling authority arrangement. Avoid diagnostic stereotypes or imitated screen dialogue. |
| **Carmine Falcone — family organization** | A community reference and obligations to an existing contact; background need not grant automatic membership. | Regular envelope plus favors and obligations; the social cost of a favor is visible. | Moderate apparent stability, high pressure from entrenched corruption and rival institutions. | Expects discretion and durable obligations; paternal presentation can conceal ruthless decisions. Continuity placement must be chosen before use. |
| **Bane — disciplined expeditionary organization** | A practical competence test, preparedness, and willingness to follow a declared operational objective. | Clear operation share and limited recovery support tied to agreed duties. | High-intensity assignments and strong exposure; worker safety depends on objectives, not just pay. | Expects discipline and competence; strategic, demanding, intolerant of careless execution. This proposes a work culture, not a new substance or combat system. |
| **Poison Ivy — proposed botanical support network** | Relevant technical/stewardship competence and a conditional referral; no required exposure or coerced initiation. | A defined service fee or practical support, with promised benefits distinguished from verified ones. | Fictional botanical exposure, coercive pressure and conflicts with civilian welfare. | Ecological priorities can override ordinary work; useful cooperation and freely chosen loyalty must be recorded separately from influence. |
| **Scarecrow — proposed fear-based operation cell** | A conditional technical/support offer with signaled hazards; refusing an unsafe trial preserves alternatives. | A stated task fee; claimed treatment or protection is not guaranteed compensation or the only recovery route. | Extreme fictional fear exposure, manipulation and exploitation of private information. | Values control of fear and experimental results over worker welfare; specific unethical conduct, not mental illness, defines the danger. |

## Advancement, consequences, and distinct play

| Proposed employer | Promotion opportunities | Betrayal consequences | Unique gameplay mechanic proposal | Law enforcement and Batman interaction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Penguin | Trusted scheduler, specialist, or delegated business operator; authority brings payroll and confidentiality obligations. | Loss of references, financial leverage, access restrictions, or an exposed confidence; not instant global hostility. | Negotiate conflicting service commitments and decide which information to verify, sell, or protect. | Corruption can obscure a specific inquiry, never erase all evidence; Batman's information gathering threatens the network. |
| Joker | Temporary responsibility or personal attention, neither a reliable safety guarantee. | Credible threats, an unsafe assignment, or abandonment of an existing promise, with signaled escape alternatives. | Interpret a changing brief, protect coworkers, and decide when an offered bonus is no longer worth the risk. Variation must be seeded and explainable after the fact. | Public spectacle raises specific investigative attention; the player can refuse to be a decoy. Batman is a survival pressure, not a farmable mission boss. |
| Black Mask | Supervisor or collector with real obligations to subordinates and an exit cost. | A contact refuses help, a debt is invoked, or a targeted coercion episode occurs; outcomes require witnesses or knowledge. | Choose whether to enforce an unfair order, conceal a coworker's shortfall, or negotiate accountability upward. | Intimidation creates witnesses and personal grudges; fear changes testimony or cooperation rather than magically deleting case facts. |
| Two-Face | Stewardship of terms or dispute resolution, granting access to challenge a process. | A declared hearing or arbitrary reinterpretation creates a contestable obligation; the player can preserve evidence of promises. | Decide whether to accept a transparent coin-dependent clause, pay for certainty, or walk away. Commit the seeded outcome once; menus cannot reroll it. | Criminal-law knowledge affects responses, not immunity. Batman can disrupt the process; the worker must choose between obedience and safe departure. |
| Riddler | Planner or technical lead with responsibility for another worker's contribution. | Loss of credit, a tainted reference, or exposure of the player's involvement. | Resolve new constraints, preserve authorship evidence, and choose between an elegant demonstration and a safer practical solution. No recycled comic riddles. | Deliberate clues create exposure. The player may protect a coworker without supporting the employer's desire to attract Batman. |
| Mr. Freeze | Trusted technician or logistics coordinator; access increases confidentiality and care obligations. | Withdrawal of access, loss of a contract, or a targeted pursuit of missing resources. | Balance safe handling, a colleague's welfare, and an urgent research dependency; technical success does not settle the ethical decision. | A sourced hazard warning or an emergency can change intervention; police and Batman know documented acts, not every hidden objective. |
| Scarface / Ventriloquist | Trusted intermediary or foreman able to clarify or exploit command ambiguity. | An instruction is denied, an associate blames the worker, or a share is withheld; preserve who heard which order. | Track authority and witness chains, seek confirmation, and decide whether protecting an intermediary matters more than official approval. | Conflicting testimony creates an information problem. Batman's scrutiny threatens the chain of intermediaries; no diagnosis determines guilt or violence. |
| Falcone | Sponsored specialist or lieutenant with competing obligations to workers, sponsors, and family leadership. | Social isolation, a called-in favor, or a targeted loss of work rather than a citywide memory reset. | Manage favors as specific reciprocal promises, including refusal and noncriminal alternatives; a community is more than a resource meter. | Institutional influence complicates an inquiry but cannot guarantee protection. Protecting a civilian contact may conflict with a sponsor's order. |
| Bane | Team specialist or planning responsibility, making preparation and retreat decisions consequential. | Loss of command trust, reassignment, or pursuit tied to a known breach. | Choose readiness, a bounded objective, and extraction commitments; eventual crew cooperation depends on declared roles, not bigger enemy statistics. | Attention tracks the operation's observed footprint. Facing Batman calls for survival or withdrawal; no routine worker duel proves superiority. |
| Poison Ivy | Trusted caretaker or technical coordinator; authority increases stewardship and consent obligations. | Lost access, disputed commitments or a bounded coercion episode; no automatic permanent devotion or removal of player input. | Choose ecological work, worker safety, independent review or departure; persist exposure, voluntary allegiance and recovery separately. | Documented harm/exposure informs a specific inquiry; Batman intervention can prioritize survival and rescue, not treat every affected worker as a willing accomplice. |
| Scarecrow | Technical responsibility with higher exposure and complicity; no promotion unlocks a mandatory cure. | A known threat, misuse of a confidence or loss of work, with independent support and escape/review routes. | Question a claimed remedy, protect a coworker, choose an ethical referral or refuse an experiment; seeded fear effects remain bounded and auditable. | Witnessed acts and specific evidence matter. Batman is a high-threat disruption to the operation; helping another worker can matter more than employer approval. |

## Proposed mechanical implications

These cultures should emerge from configurable employer goals, compensation
terms, knowledge, refusal rules, and consequences. Economic coefficients alone
are insufficient. Do not set `if name == "Joker"` in an engine reducer or turn
an employer's advertised personality into an unverified canonical fact.

Personal loyalty can conflict with employer approval. A worker may leave one
organization while retaining a friend or a debt. Recruiters can lie, but the UI
must preserve the source and uncertainty of a promise. Trait combinations need
author review so "unpredictable" does not mean unexplained arbitrary punishment.

All eleven employers use [conditional opportunities](GOON_CONDITIONAL_OPPORTUNITIES.md),
not guaranteed unlocks. Recruitment, wages and personal devotion are separate
from faction economics and [influence conditions](GOON_VILLAIN_INFLUENCE_SYSTEM.md).
Every proposed culture inherits the recruitment/culture and employment rows of
the [ten-field review](GOON_GAMEPLAY_PRINCIPLES.md#the-ten-field-design-review),
including persistent alternatives, grind risks, migration and agency/exploit checks.
No special mechanic above authorizes a new subsystem during M013.1 or M014.

## Paper comparison scenario

**Proposal, not an executable mission:** an ordinary coworker asks the player to
help finish a damaged delivery before accepting a new assignment. In Penguin's
network the conflict is a negotiated service commitment; in Black Mask's outfit
it is a coercive order; in a Riddler cell it can become a dispute over credit and
proof. Saving the coworker's job, accepting the assignment, seeking a substitute,
and leaving work must alter later access or relationships. If only pay and enemy
numbers change between versions, the employer design has failed its purpose.
