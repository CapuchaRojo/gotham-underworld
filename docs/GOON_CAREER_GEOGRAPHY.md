# Gotham: The Goon — Career Geography

**Status: original future design proposals, unimplemented.** The
[career web](GOON_CRIMINAL_CAREER_WEB.md) defines flexible work, not rigid classes.
Use the [atlas](GOON_GOTHAM_ATLAS.md), [place registry](GOON_LOCATION_REGISTRY.md),
[travel contract](GOON_TRAVEL_NETWORK.md) and
[conditional opportunities](GOON_CONDITIONAL_OPPORTUNITIES.md) to turn location
into competing obligations, knowledge and access rather than extra walking clicks.

## Civilian geography is part of the game

Housing is a relationship to a property, not ownership of an entire neighborhood.
Affordable rooms, cooperatives, temporary shelter and supported-housing referrals
provide different obligations with viable zero-cash support. Home location can
change who can help and which work conflicts with care, without adding daily rent
grind or a survival-meter death spiral in this milestone.

Care is distributed: hospitals, community clinics, voluntary counseling, advocacy
and reentry offices appear in several regions. Ethical improvement must not depend
on reaching Arkham, joining a gang or meeting a villain. Visiting a public clinic
does not admit the player, advance a legal case or reveal another person's records.
Social venues, parks, libraries, schools and ordinary shops support friendship,
refusal and reinvention as well as work.

Minor crews and independent specialists can operate across several places without
each gaining a district treasury. A known contact's usual workplace is an imperfect
observation, not omniscient tracking. The [rogue registry](GOON_ROGUES_GALLERY_REGISTRY.md)
supplies optional versioned content. Arkham's presence on the map does not guarantee
Joker recruitment, and missing that arc cannot prevent a completed civilian or
independent career.

## Worked paper scenarios

**All scenarios are design exercises, not playable missions or executed tests.**

1. **Two shores, one promise.** A driver in n13 considers work in n31. The proposed
   bus round trip costs 16 TU, while walking both ways exceeds 18. Keeping the bus
   plan preserves a pickup commitment; taking a local shift preserves flexibility;
   accepting a revised remote contract after an observed closure can lose a bonus
   but keep a coworker's trust. The decision concerns promises and access, not
   repeated waiting clicks. Public-transport fares are zero in the minimum proposal.
2. **A borrowed introduction.** A fixer in Market Steps hears that a rogue is
   working at Amusement Mile. Verify through a consenting local contact, spend
   travel TU on an uncertain lead, or keep an existing client appointment. The
   source can be wrong; lost time, a kept appointment or damaged confidence persists.
   No pin spawns a villain and an introduction is not formal membership.
3. **Care near home.** A worker returns to Narrows with a fictional exposure and
   an unsafe employer demand. Choose local voluntary care/reentry support, keep
   contact with a trusted coworker, or pursue a dubious referral after a warning.
   Improvement, confidentiality and work access can diverge; an Arkham storyline
   remains optional. Lawful support and ending a contract do not erase victims or
   existing legal obligations.

## Ten-field review and shallow areas

| Criterion | Career-geography contract |
| --- | --- |
| Decisions | Choose work, housing/support, travel, verification, refusal and competing commitments |
| Feedback | Show actual contract terms, known access/commute and affected people/services |
| Persistence | Specific relationships, promises, condition knowledge, work outcomes and optional approved property/care state |
| Alternatives | Local/civilian work, changed appointment, help, negotiated substitute or lawful exit |
| Replayability | Demand, contacts, neighborhood incidents, information quality and personal obligations |
| Grind risk | Identical errands, service visits, commute/XP farming; distinct consequences and useful local options are required |
| Determinism | Stable location/opportunity IDs, pinned terms, recorded choices and exactly-once outcomes |
| Save compatibility | Existing careers gain no invented home, job location, diagnosis or missed appointment; opt-in activation only |
| Offline | Local definitions, route knowledge, finite authored scenarios and complete support paths |
| Agency / exploits | No location-based guilt, coerced allegiance, forced violent class, unlimited referral rewards or unavailable-villain softlock |

The minimum remains shallow if places differ only in payout, travel cost or map
color. The table alone does not supply mission variety, civilian payroll, living
NPC schedules or personalities. Future scenario review must prove the same task
changes its decision when obligations/knowledge change, and test refusal, zero
cash, unavailable contacts, failed travel, lawful support, migration and deterministic
resume. This documentation adds no new playable decisions or persistent effects.
