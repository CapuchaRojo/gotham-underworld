# Gotham: The Goon — Villain Influence and Recovery

**Status: unimplemented M013.1 design proposal.** No current health, dependency,
conditioning, psychiatric or influence mechanics exist. This is fictional Gotham
story design, not a clinical model or treatment instructions. No real substances,
doses, delivery methods or chemical recipes are specified. These systems are
outside the proposed minimal M014 combat scope.

Read alongside [institutions and recovery](GOON_INSTITUTIONS_AND_RECOVERY.md),
[conditional opportunities](GOON_CONDITIONAL_OPPORTUNITIES.md), and the
[career web's worked scenarios](GOON_CRIMINAL_CAREER_WEB.md#three-worked-divergent-career-scenarios).

## Separate exposure, symptoms, pressure, and allegiance

An influence condition records what happened to a person; it is not their moral
identity. Do not use a single sanity, corruption or devotion meter. Proposed
condition definitions distinguish these dimensions:

| Dimension | Proposed fictional representation | Essential distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical exposure | Source incident, actor, exposure tag, observed effects and uncertain observations | An exposure is not proof of criminal intent, consent or a diagnosis |
| Fear conditioning | Specific trigger associations, stress cues and coping/support options | Fear is not violence, cowardice or allegiance |
| Chemical/botanical influence | Explicitly fictional effect policy, severity and review state | No real pharmacology; do not imply all affected people behave identically |
| Dependency / withdrawal | Fictional recurring urges, disruption and requested support | Discomfort does not force crime or imply a defective character |
| Compromised judgment | Known information limitations, perceived pressure and decision prompts | Altered perspective is not permission for hidden irreversible player actions |
| Coercion | Threat/source, constrained choices, consent boundaries and available help | Compliance under pressure is not freely chosen loyalty |
| Voluntary allegiance | Separately recorded, informed choice with review/exit possibilities | Never inferred from symptoms, affinity, attraction or time exposed |
| Recovery / resistance | Coping milestones, trusted support, reduced impact and specific learned resistance | Improvement need not erase memory or grant universal immunity |
| Long-term vulnerability | A specific residual trigger with warnings and a management route | No inevitable permanent decline or surprise irreversible relapse |

Future state could use stable condition IDs, versioned definitions, source event,
actor, severity 0–3, trigger tags, support choices, next review day, resolution and
last processed marker. Those ranges are draft game parameters, not medical
measurements. Conditions are bounded; repeated contact updates an existing
condition under explicit rules rather than stacking unlimited penalties.
Historical saves gain no inferred symptoms, exposures or allegiance.

## Agency contract

Before a risky fictional encounter, communicate known hazards and uncertainty.
When influence starts, show the source where known, current limitations and at
least two useful responses, including seeking help or a route toward safety.
Never present the narrator's manipulated claim as trustworthy objective UI state.

Temporary manipulation can affect perceived information, dialogue options or
bounded task pressure while preserving player interaction. The player can
question a promise, contact a chosen person, resist a demand, negotiate delay,
protect someone, seek an independent opinion or attempt to leave. A constrained
episode needs a finite authored resolution/review point and an always-available
option to summarize distressing material and advance to a safe decision boundary.
Skipping presentation preserves the chosen consequence; it cannot become a
reroll or magically remove every obligation.

Do not secretly spend the player's money, choose lethal violence, select intimate
behavior, pledge permanent allegiance, delete a career, or assign a psychiatric
identity through an automatic influence roll. Irreversible choices require an
explicit player decision with legible stakes. Narrated urges are not commands
already committed by the simulation. Strong coercion may be dangerous in fiction;
it must not become an indefinite lockout of input, saving, or recovery.

Persistent influence can remain unresolved for a time while the player still
chooses how to work, whom to trust and when to seek help. Its availability may
close an unsafe contract or open a support opportunity, without forcing a named
villain encounter or making care inaccessible. A prior voluntary alliance must
not retroactively legitimize a later coerced demand, and recovery need not imply
choosing enemies on the player's behalf.

## Illustrative Poison Ivy branches

DC's character profile supplies the broad botanical/pheromone influence motif;
the following condition paths and workplace scenarios are original proposals,
not canonical biology or a fixed portrayal. See the
[official Poison Ivy reference](https://www.dc.com/characters/poison-ivy).

| Branch | Player interaction and feedback | Persistent consequence / next possibilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resist at first contact | Choose a warned resistance/exit response; show the one committed result | No condition, or a bounded residual effect if declared; do not award repeated resistance XP |
| Escape with susceptibility | Contact a trusted worker and leave the source | Specific trigger/vulnerability and a known support route; work can continue with disclosed limitations |
| Recurring cravings or triggers | Choose support, a safer routine or a review; display uncertainty and missed obligations | Coping milestones and relationship consequences; no automatic villain loyalty |
| Temporary manipulation | Question a request, seek corroboration or protect another worker | A bounded pressure episode, recorded choices and a finite review point |
| Remain influenced | Decline immediate care while retaining contact and exit options | An unresolved condition with recurring meaningful decisions; no endless forced chores |
| Seek treatment | Choose an ethical independent practitioner, consent boundaries and practical support | Credible improvement and alternative employment access; arrest is not required |
| Consider a dubious specialist | Examine a referral's source/conflicts, refuse, or request another opinion | A conditional Strange/Scarecrow story may become eligible; no guaranteed meeting or exclusive cure |
| Develop partial resistance / help others | Apply a learned coping milestone or support a coworker's own decision | Reduced impact for a specific trigger, a remembered favor and possible support work; no universal immunity |

Use the [opportunity contract](GOON_CONDITIONAL_OPPORTUNITIES.md) for every referral.
Skills and support can change risk but cannot guarantee immunity or a personal
audience with Ivy. Separate the player's voluntary opinion of her cause from a
condition's coercive effect. Neither a resistance failure nor a recovery choice
is a romance/consent flag.

## Recovery, resistance, setbacks, and exploitation

Ethical care must offer a reachable improvement path: informed consent, goals the
player helps choose, confidential support, reduced fictional symptoms, better
coping, repaired commitments or access to work. Do not promise an instant universal
cure. Do not secretly make every ethical practitioner ineffective. A zero-cash
basic support route and an alternative referral must exist even after a bad
encounter or lost housing. No villain-exclusive antidote is the only ending path.

Recovery milestones come from distinct choices and support, not buying affection,
repeating appointments indefinitely, intentionally seeking exposure or enduring
real-time timers. Proposed daily processing applies at most once per condition
per advanced day; it records scheduled reviews and bounded changes. A refusal
of one form of care can lead to another, not mandatory deterioration. Setbacks
need a recorded cause, a warning where knowable and a revised plan; they cannot
erase all progress or turn every previous choice into a false promise.

Learned resistance is specific and capped. Re-exposure carries harm and never
provides a profitable qualification/resistance farm. A new exposure cannot
retroactively change an earlier committed outcome. Helping another affected
person respects their consent; the player does not acquire ownership or loyalty.

Scarecrow can provide a character-specific fear/exploitation risk, while an
eligible Hugo Strange episode can involve misused authority or information.
Neither is a necessary recovery specialist or representative of ordinary care.
Scarecrow's broad fear-based methods are anchored in
[DC's profile](https://www.dc.com/characters/scarecrow); Strange's institutional
role varies by continuity, as illustrated by
[DC's discussion of the Arkham games](https://www.dc.com/blog/2022/03/18/the-worst-mayors-of-gotham-city-ever).
No plot, experiment, dialogue or diagnostic language from those sources is adapted.

## Treatment, accountability, and legal facts

Observed acts, source reliability, coercion evidence, clinical support and legal
responsibility are separate records. Treatment does not delete a victim's account,
automatically grant immunity, increase guilt, or justify arrest. A future fictional
court can consider documented coercion through explicit rules and review; no
present case is rewritten because this design exists.

Mental-health conditions are not a predictor of villainy. Ethical practitioners,
ordinary patients, family and peer support have independent goals. Avoid turning
diagnoses into enemy classes or treating dependency as a character's entire
personality. Distress and horror can exist without humiliation or a blanket
claim that seeking treatment is dangerous.

## Ten-field review

| Criterion | Influence contract |
| --- | --- |
| Meaningful decisions | Resist, leave, seek support, question a demand, choose care, maintain a contact or support another person. |
| Observable consequences | Distinguish reported experience, known limitations, uncertainty, chosen consent and actual improvement. |
| Persistent state | Exposure provenance, bounded condition/trigger state, chosen allegiance separately, milestones and review/settlement markers. |
| Alternative outcomes | No effect, residual vulnerability, bounded manipulation, unresolved condition, credible recovery, or freely chosen affiliation. |
| Replayability | Different sources, prior trust, coping milestones, work obligations and care choices change the response. |
| Grind risk | Appointment spam, exposure farming or recurring scripted urges; use distinct finite milestones and useful alternatives. |
| Determinism | Isolated exposure/episode RNG, stable IDs, saved decisions and once-per-day condition processing; views never reroll. |
| Save compatibility | Later additive versioned empty condition state; no diagnosis/history invented for old saves; pin active episode and recovery rules. |
| Offline operation | Local narrative/policies and explicit game-day reviews, no real-time service or AI therapist. |
| Exploits and agency | Prevent infinite stacking, duplicate recovery grants, forced irreversible acts, false consent and an unavoidable care softlock. |

Future tests must cover all branches, a condition persisting without removing
useful choices, repeat-trigger idempotency, no profitable re-exposure, zero-cash
improvement, corrupt-provider exits, consent/affiliation separation, rejected
commands, malformed saves and deterministic resume. They are requirements for a
later approved milestone, not executed influence tests in M013.1.
