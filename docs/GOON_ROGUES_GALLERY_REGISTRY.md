# Gotham: The Goon — Proposed Rogues Gallery Registry

**Status: M013.1 content-design proposals, not an implemented roster.** These
The original 35 entries are retained; Mission 029 adds Jane Doe for **36 research
entries**. Calendar Man and Crazy-Quilt already existed and are enriched, not
counted again. The Terrible Trio is a group containing
three people. Listing a person does not create a faction, district, encounter,
employment offer or guarantee of presence. No universal DC continuity is selected.
The [minor ecosystem](GOON_MINOR_CRIMINAL_ECOSYSTEM.md) defines operating scales;
[conditional opportunities](GOON_CONDITIONAL_OPPORTUNITIES.md) govern availability.

## Evidence and identity rules

- **Reference / canon anchor** names a published work or continuity and supplies
  a conservative factual motif, identity or capability. Publisher profiles and
  solicitations are preferred. Links explicitly marked **secondary** are factual
  indexes/encyclopedias used where an accessible publisher account was sparse;
  they are not DC endorsement or evidence that every comic panel was inspected.
- These are research anchors, not a completed continuity audit. Reconcile the
  actual chosen editions before content approval, especially identities, deaths,
  powers and relationships. Unknown identities stay unknown. Modern publisher
  profiles sometimes discuss several eras; do not import every ability together.
- **Original proposal** covers operating scale, role, specific behavior, jobs,
  faction ties and player consequences below, unless explicitly placed in the
  canon field. Those proposals do not adapt fan-created characterization, dialogue,
  narrator casts, published scenes or third-party mission plots.
- Proposed IDs identify a particular version/persona, not a display label or
  current save key. Distinct mantle holders require distinct IDs. A later campaign
  must bind an approved compatible subset to its pinned continuity and availability.
  An actor recorded dead/retired is not silently respawned to complete a checklist.
- Boss, contractor, associate, rival and wildcard are contextual roles. A boss can
  lead five people without owning a district. Power, personality, fame and operating
  scale are separate. No diagnosis, disability or appearance determines villainy.

Sources consulted November 3, 2026. Facts are paraphrased; no dialogue, artwork,
scripts, recipes, technical criminal methods or character stat blocks are copied.

Mission 029 sources were rechecked November 5, 2026. This document owns detailed
minor-character identity/source records; the [encounter design](GOON_ROGUE_ENCOUNTERS_DESIGN.md)
owns proposed presence/difficulty/access for its overlapping selection, not a second
identity authority. Its current 36 individual/mantle roles are a different set
from these 36 research entries. Neither count reinstates excluded Professor Milo.
The [employer matrix](GOON_FACTION_EMPLOYER_MATRIX.md#mission-029-organized-crime-candidate-registry)
owns organization candidates and evidence; a character's network does not create
another copy of that person or require district ownership.

## 01. Killer Moth

- **ID / identity:** `dc.character.killer_moth.drury_walker`; Drury Walker, with
  Cameron Van Cleer as an alias in the cited comics lineage.
- **Reference / canon anchor:** Batman #63 and the later Walker lineage;
  [DC's mantle overview](https://www.dc.com/blog/2017/04/26/miles-to-go-the-un-familiar-faces-of-all-star-batman)
  and [secondary identity index](https://dcuguide.com/Charaxes_%28Drury_Walker%29).
  Costumed criminal protection and moth-themed equipment; later Charaxes is a
  different transformation state, not a default ability of this proposal.
- **Original proposal — scale / role:** independent contractor or small-crew boss.
- **Original proposal — skills:** equipment appraisal, contract judgment and crew coordination.
- **Behavior / work:** demands recognition for reliable protection; jobs involve
  disputed escort terms, equipment responsibility and a coworker's unsafe order.
- **Faction ties:** Penguin might buy a service; Black Mask might pressure the
  contract. Neither is a standing canonical alliance asserted here.
- **Player consequences:** a kept protection promise earns a reference; abandoning
  a worker damages trust and can expose documented involvement.

## Adoption and gameplay review

These entries provide thematic hooks, not completed NPC personalities. Every
authored encounter still needs the [ten-field review](GOON_GAMEPLAY_PRINCIPLES.md#the-ten-field-design-review):
choices, visible consequences, persistent state, alternatives, replayability,
grind risks, determinism, save compatibility, offline play and agency/exploit concerns.
Registry presence cannot substitute for consent, knowledge, legal process or an
actual invitation. The [Joker contract](GOON_JOKER_RECRUITMENT.md) is a separate
original campaign rule, not a loophole granted by association with any rogue.

Proposed variety comes from conflicting work terms, credit, authority, knowledge,
ordinary obligations and different character goals, not dozens of differently
named reward multipliers. Avoid making every cameo a combat gate or collection
achievement. Repeated introductions, favors and alias swaps must not generate
free money, trust, membership or a fresh identity after arrest/death.

A future content review must select versions, inspect the cited primary works,
resolve secondary-source ambiguities, define unavailable/deceased states, validate
actor/persona/group references and approve original scenarios. No mandatory
online lookup belongs in gameplay; ship approved local definitions and provenance.
Historical saves keep their original people, faction keys and memories; this
document adds no save fields or roster migration. No named content pack, assets,
public distribution or M014 implementation is authorized here.
