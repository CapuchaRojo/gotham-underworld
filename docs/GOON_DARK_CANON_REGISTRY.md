# Gotham: The Goon — DARK Character Canon and Continuity Registry

**Mission 029 addendum; research/design only, November 5, 2026.**
This is the detailed authority for the seven newly requested DARK candidates
and limited bridge notes below. The [master index](GOON_MASTER_ROSTER.md) points
here; existing rogue identities remain in their original registries. No actor,
power, organization, command, save field or supernatural encounter is implemented.

## Evidence and identity policy

**Canon anchor** means a narrow fact supported by the linked publisher profile,
collection, solicitation or commentary. Publisher profiles can compress eras;
solicitations are not full stories. **Continuity-specific** facts are not combined
into one biography. **Proposal** labels all availability, jobs, knowledge rules,
consent policy, costs and progression. Confidence is high for the cited narrow
premises, not for an unselected complete continuity. Some direct pages returned
403 after indexed text was available; no complete comic, film or episode was read.
No scripts, incantations, dialogue, panels or published plot chains are copied.

“DARK” is our optional narrative category, not a canonical organization, moral
alignment, player class or mandatory team. Justice League Dark has particular
published rosters; membership in one version does not make all seven members,
allies, villains or residents of Gotham. Related-character cards are not alliance
proof. Occult expertise, appearance, disability and dangerous abilities do not
determine morality or criminal guilt.

## D01. Madame Xanadu

- **Identity / proposed ID:** Madame Xanadu; Nimue Inwudu in the cited Vertigo
  account. `dc.character.madame_xanadu.comics_candidate`.
- **Canon motivation / abilities:** divination and intervention in magical
  threats; the 2008-series account uses tarot and Arthurian history. Its loss of
  earlier godlike power is a specific history, not infinite present capability.
  [DC's Vertigo discussion](https://www.dc.com/blog/2018/06/19/vertigo-book-club-madame-xanadu-unites-a-universe).
- **Allegiances / differences:** a published Justice League Dark association,
  not a universal commander of occult people ([2011-era team material](https://www.dc.com/blog/2011/06/27/dc-comics-the-new-52-justice-league-dark)).
  The blind sorceress in the 2019 Swamp Thing TV adaptation must not be substituted
  for every comics portrayal; [DC distinguishes the accounts](https://www.dc.com/blog/2018/10/26/jeryl-prescott-joins-swamp-thing-as-madame-xanadu).
  Arthurian connections do not authorize importing another character's entire era.
- **Limits:** no universal divination range, guaranteed correct interpretation or
  availability is established here. A vision is a bounded source whose content
  and uncertainty need explicit authorship; it is not a query of the career save.
- **Proposal:** late optional adviser, not a villain or repeatable fortune machine.
  A credible referral may earn a consultation; warnings, refusal and an ordinary
  advocate remain alternatives. No guaranteed meeting, rescue or membership.

## D02. Zatanna Zatara

- **Identity / proposed ID:** Zatanna Zatara / Zatanna.
  `dc.character.zatanna.zatara`.
- **Canon motivation / abilities:** stage performance and powerful magic,
  typically expressed through backward speech; opposition to harmful magic and
  her father's loss inform her portrayal. [DC profile](https://www.dc.com/characters/zatanna).
- **Allegiances / differences:** Justice League participation in the cited era;
  Justice League Dark appearances are version-bound, not permanent employment
  of every magician. Father Zatara is a relationship anchor, not a second Zatanna.
  The [2025 series account](https://www.dc.com/blog/2025/02/24/zatanna-1-showcases-the-mistress-of-magic-at-her-best)
  explicitly includes a sign-language spell: silence is **not a universal
  canonical off-switch**. Do not mix that portrayal with incompatible eras.
- **Limits:** the profile's broad power language is not a guaranteed solution to
  every problem. Exact spell constraints require the selected edition; attention,
  informed choice and safe participation still matter. No new universal mana
  formula or invented inability is asserted as lore.
- **Proposal:** late optional protector/consultant. Lawful stage work can furnish
  an ordinary reference; the contact must actually transmit a later relevant lead.
  No compulsory romance, apprenticeship, personal encounter or free life reset.

## D03. John Constantine

- **Identity / proposed ID:** John Constantine / Hellblazer (title/epithet, not
  another actor). `dc.character.john_constantine.comics_candidate`.
- **Canon motivation / abilities:** occult investigation, sorcery, deception and
  costly dealings; morally compromised conduct coexists with efforts to protect
  others. Powerful enemies and a damaging history constrain easy trust.
  [DC profile](https://www.dc.com/characters/john-constantine).
- **Allegiances / differences:** Hellblazer, shared-universe team stories and TV
  portrayals require distinct era decisions. A Justice League Dark appearance
  does not make him a reliable subordinate, ubiquitous Gotham resident or leader
  of all occult actors. [Specific team issue](https://www.dc.com/comics/justice-league-dark-2011/justice-league-dark-10).
- **Limits:** knowledge, bargaining and magic are not universal truth access,
  invulnerability or freedom from consequences. No particular demon, pact,
  romantic relationship or guaranteed betrayal is bound by this proposal.
- **Proposal:** ambiguous investigator/intermediary. Disclose knowable obligations
  and permit independent advice, refusal and bounded exit. Do not make an opaque
  eternal soul-debt or forced atrocity the price of ordinary survival.

## D04. Jason Blood / Etrigan

- **Identity / proposed binding:** `dc.binding.jason_blood_etrigan.comics_candidate`
  references Jason Blood and Etrigan as **distinct bound wills**, not interchangeable
  aliases or a clinical diagnosis. No independently duplicated contract payout
  or transformed-body budget is implied.
- **Canon motivation / abilities:** Merlin's binding links a long-lived human
  occult figure and a powerful demon. Their purposes can conflict.
  [DC's Kirby-era collection](https://www.dc.com/graphic-novels/dc-finest-the-demon-birth-of-the-demon).
  DC also describes Etrigan's demon flame and formidable physical opposition in
  a particular encounter ([publisher retrospective](https://www.dc.com/blog/2025-10-15/come-out-writing-stop-sleeping-on-aquaman));
  that is an ability anchor, not an imported combat scale or universal victory.
  The 2017 account has Blood trying to keep the demon contained
  ([DC preview](https://www.dc.com/blog/2017/08/16/exclusive-etrigan-emerges-again-in-the-demon-hell-is-earth)).
- **Allegiances / differences:** the [2017 animated Justice League Dark](https://www.dc.com/movies/justice-league-dark-2017)
  is a separate adaptation. A team-up is not universal allegiance, villainy or
  permission for one bound party to consent for another.
- **Limits:** transformation conditions, control, infernal powers and vulnerabilities
  must be edition-specific; no universal “summon Etrigan” option or controllable
  companion is established. Blood's information is not automatically Etrigan's
  truthful report, and neither knows every player action.
- **Proposal:** conditional late hazardous contact, consultation or avoidance.
  Safe withdrawal/civilian protection can be meaningful outcomes without combat,
  recruitment, demonic service or defeating the demon.

## D05. Swamp Thing

- **Identity / proposed ID:** `dc.mantle.swamp_thing.pending_actor`;
  actor/era deliberately unselected. Alec-Holland-related incarnations, Alex Olsen
  and Levi Kamei are **not one interchangeable person**.
- **Canon motivation / abilities:** the Alec-related profile describes a plant
  being with Holland's memories, plant control and embodiment through vegetation,
  concerned with humanity and the environment. [DC profile](https://www.dc.com/characters/swamp-thing).
- **Continuity differences:** [DC's historical survey](https://www.dc.com/blog/2023/10/05/blood-in-the-bayou-five-terrifying-swamp-thing-tales)
  distinguishes Olsen, Holland and the later “Anatomy Lesson” reinterpretation.
  [Ram V's creator interview](https://www.dc.com/blog/2022/07/12/v-for-variety-aqua-horror-swamp-things-and-batman-opera-with-ram-v)
  distinguishes Levi's human/transformed experience. A mantle-level profile's
  first-appearance date does not prove every story stars Alec.
- **Allegiances / limits:** connection to the Green is not universal loyalty to
  Poison Ivy or an all-knowing human surveillance system. A particular story
  can threaten that connection ([Justice League United #12](https://www.dc.com/comics/justice-league-united-2014/justice-league-united-12));
  no numeric range or universal weakness is invented here.
- **Proposal:** late optional guardian, not an automatic villain. Ecological harm,
  restoration or civilian protection may matter if actually observed/transmitted.
  Do not reduce a powerful being to a repeatable landlord, healer or crime eraser.

## D06. Jack Ryder / The Creeper

- **Identity / proposed ID:** Jack Ryder / The Creeper.
  `dc.character.creeper.jack_ryder.pending_origin`.
- **Canon motivation / abilities:** journalist/pundit and transformed crimefighter;
  the [2006-series collection](https://www.dc.com/graphic-novels/the-creeper-2006/the-creeper-welcome-to-creepsville)
  describes strength, agility, stamina and healing. The [first-issue solicitation](https://www.dc.com/comics/the-creeper-2006/the-creeper-1)
  frames a doctor's transformation. This supports a science-origin candidate,
  not proof of supernatural detection.
- **Allegiances / differences:** the [New 52 Creeper solicitation](https://www.dc.com/comics/justice-league-dark-2011/justice-league-dark-23-1-the-creeper)
  instead describes Ryder as an unwitting host. Exact entity/origin must be
  selected; do not attach a host-based supernatural biography to all versions.
  Newsman, unsettling hero and occult-boundary role are not automatic villainy.
- **Limits:** no immortality, universal mind-reading, magic authentication or
  Justice League Dark membership is established. Appearance and behavior do not
  diagnose a condition or prove guilt. Newsroom source limits still apply.
- **Proposal:** grounded journalist or late ambiguous incident contact depending
  on selected origin; one Ryder identity, not two reporters leaking private facts.
  Reporting/withholding/refusing is possible without discovering an occult branch.

## D07. Boston Brand / Deadman

- **Identity / proposed ID:** Boston Brand / Deadman.
  `dc.character.deadman.boston_brand`.
- **Canon motivation / abilities:** murdered aerialist seeking justice, later
  helping the living; spirit/possession powers granted by Rama Kushna in the
  publisher account. It names Batman collaborations and a Justice League Dark
  phase, not all occult actors as one organization.
  [DC profile](https://www.dc.com/characters/deadman).
- **Limits / differences:** invisibility and possession do not establish an
  observer in every room, universally believed testimony or omniscient access
  to a host's history. Communication/embodiment restrictions need an edition;
  comic and animated team histories are not silently interchangeable.
- **Proposal:** late optional witness/protector, never an automatic criminal.
  **Player-body possession requires a separately approved consent/depiction
  contract**, with refusal and withdrawal. That is our participation rule, not a
  false claim that canon possession always requires permission. No remote
  command execution, free action, erased debt or shared world-knowledge feed.

## Authority, availability and unresolved continuity choices

All seven DARK records are **unimplemented, proposed optional/conditional late
contacts**, not a guaranteed cast, employer list, power progression or faction.
Personal encounter, trust, assistance and membership are distinct. No DARK entry
belongs in the existing excluded-Milo slot or replaces an existing rogue.

The [progression contract](GOON_DARK_PROGRESSION_CONTRACT.md) owns eligibility,
consequences, recovery and approval questions. It keeps the separately protected
organization entirely outside DARK discovery. The master index is author-only,
not a player encyclopedia exposing hidden identities, relationships or future
availability. A role can remain historical, unavailable, absent or refused.

Xanadu portrayal, Blood/Etrigan binding limits, Deadman participation policy and
the actual power/knowledge constraints of each encounter. Research supports a
starting proposal; it does not settle every spell, weakness, alliance or death.
