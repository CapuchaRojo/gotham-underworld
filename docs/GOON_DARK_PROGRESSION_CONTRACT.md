# Gotham: The Goon — Optional DARK Discovery and Eligibility Contract

**Mission 029 addendum; proposed narrative/system design only.**
No gameplay, schema, event ID, RNG, navigation, UI or mandatory campaign changes.
The [canon registry](GOON_DARK_CANON_REGISTRY.md) owns character evidence; the
[master index](GOON_MASTER_ROSTER.md) is an authoring navigation aid, not player
knowledge. “DARK” does not mean villain, class, team membership or morality score.

## 1. Grounded-first scope and separate consent

A complete lawful, criminal, reform, neutral or survival career can remain
grounded with **no supernatural involvement**. No occult trial, exposure, injury,
debt, achievement or famous contact is required for housing, work, legal recovery,
an ordinary ending or advancement. Not participating is a substantive option,
not a missed “true ending.” No supernatural catastrophe taxes a grounded career
merely for opting out.

Proposed future content policy: separately approve a late optional content pack
and explicit player participation controls. A world incident may originate and
resolve independently under that pack; discovery and participation remain separate.
Refusal does not rewrite established world truth or convert an event into a fake.
Grounded-mode projection withholds unsolicited occult offers and compulsory
occult effects. No current settings, world facts or historical saves are changed.
Exact pack-selection/consent UX needs approval before implementation.

A grounded criminal is not immune to ordinary police or vigilante consequences
under their own approved systems. Opting out only prevents forced supernatural
participation; it does not erase observed harm or override ordinary legal rules.
Occult intervention belongs to the selected, consented content scope, never an
extra punishment for refusing the branch.

Preserve the original goon perspective: ordinary obligations still matter.
Powerful allies are neither general services nor mechanisms to conjure wages,
erase rent/debt, cancel warrants, compel employers, resurrect contacts or replace
the legal/career engine. This is a scope/availability rule, not a claim that every
canonical magician lacks such capabilities. Any exceptional authored help needs
specific motive, evidence, limits and consequences without making basic survival
depend on it.

## 2. Eligibility and lifecycle — proposed, not executable fields

| Stage | Required fact / distinction | Failure or refusal alternative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selected world content | Approved compatible continuity, actor availability, independent incident cause and pack adoption | No pack means no new occult state/effects or invented historical incidents |
| Late meaningful progression | Completed grounded responsibility/career arc and a credible relationship/investigative responsibility; equivalent lawful/criminal routes | No calendar-day, wealth, crime-count or repetitive-shift gate; exact milestone set remains unapproved |
| Discoverable lead | Actually observed discrepancy/document/witness plus a credible channel available at that time | False/stale/incomplete information may remain unverified; no source means no discovery |
| Invitation to inquire | A bounded opportunity, known risks/terms, safe exit and an available source | Inspect, ask for ordinary help, postpone, decline or miss; no forced occult meeting |
| Explicit involvement | Player opts into the specific role; execution-time location/access, action/TU, custody and funding checks | Reject atomically without state/RNG/budget/knowledge changes |
| Investigation / response | Distinguish world truth, observations, interpretation, source credibility, transmitted claims and actual recipients | Partial findings, refusal, error, withdrawal, betrayal or missed deadline remain authored outcomes |
| Independent resolution | One cause-backed terminal transition/effect receipt, whether player attends or not | No duplicate reward, endless replay, compulsory rescue or last-minute forced recruitment |
| Aftermath / grounded return | Known relationships, accepted obligations and specific resource/legal effects persist through their existing authorities | Accessible ordinary work/home/support remains; no permanent hero/villain lock from survival or failure |

“Late” is deliberately not the protected organization's gate and grants no access
to that organization. The precise DARK milestone threshold is an approval item,
not permission to implement a new universal progression counter.

## 3. Credible alternate discovery routes

All are **original future proposals**, available only after the approved late/
consent gate. Each needs a real source, finite opportunity and non-occult exit.
No route guarantees any particular famous person, truth, allegiance or reward.

| Route | Causal lead / meaningful choice | Lasting possibilities without universal knowledge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Investigation | Compare a received document with a credible witness/record; choose whether to verify, transmit or withhold | Preserve provenance; a correction reaches only its recipients; an unresolved ordinary explanation stays viable |
| Relationships | A contact who actually received information offers a cautious referral; choose confidentiality, corroboration or refusal | Specific trust/obligation changes; Tess/Cal do not become occult experts by author fiat |
| Protecting civilians | Help people reach safety from an independently caused incident; decide who receives a sourced account | A witness may later recommend the player; protective help earns no automatic hero faction or occult sensitivity |
| Lawful work | A genuine stage/service obligation yields a dated unusual observation; choose an ordinary complaint or a consented specialist inquiry | Work can be completed and paid under ordinary terms without meeting Zatanna, discovering magic or accepting a second contract |
| Responsible reform | Repair a specifically witnessed harm, return entrusted material, keep a promise or support someone leaving coercion | An informed actor may offer bounded advice/protection; no automatic forgiveness, exoneration or global “good” meter |
| Neutral survival | Leave danger, seek an ethical provider, decline information-sharing or remain uninvolved | Basic recovery/ordinary careers continue; no occult reward or penalty for declining |

A frightening experience alone does not prove supernatural truth. Fear-toxin
effects, uncertainty, trauma, mental distress and someone's claimed authority
are not magic detectors. Sources/actors can be wrong without invalidating the
player's chosen safety response. No real criminal, medical or ritual instructions.

## 4. Four encounters — optional special achievement, never the only entrance

The user's concept is retained as a **proposed optional four-encounter achievement**
with working label **Four Ways Back**; name/reward/availability require approval.
It is not a boss ladder, universal unlock, mandatory checklist or faction pledge.
Other discovery routes work without any of the four.

| Encounter | Qualifying original outcome idea | Evidence / anti-farming boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Escape Poison Ivy | Leave an actual unsafe demand/exposure or arrange a safe civilian exit | Real bounded incident and exit receipt; no reward for provoking exposure, defeating Ivy or repeatedly leaving the same place |
| Survive Scarecrow | Reach safety, assist an evacuation or obtain ordinary support after a real incident | Source-backed outcome, not a fear-meter threshold; no requirement to relive distress, fight or prove magic |
| Overcome Deacon Blackfire | Refuse/break a specific coercive obligation, help someone leave or responsibly expose a claim | A witnessed safety/agency result, not killing, conversion, combat strength or harming vulnerable followers |
| Avoid Hugo Strange | Identify a credibly unsafe offered referral and choose an ethical alternate provider/advocate | Actual offered hazard, prior information and refusal/alternate receipt; never infer avoidance from “has not met Strange” |

At most one distinct qualifying outcome per encounter identity/cause; transformations,
aliases, replays, previews, reloads and source retellings cannot mint new credit.
No repeated shift/favor/commute grind, real-time timer or consumable payment.
Four receipts would grant only a proposed private commemorative journal entry,
not money, immunity, magic powers, a canonical truth claim or a mandatory quest.
The entry itself follows consent/knowledge limits. Partial completion remains
valid; dangerous encounters are never scheduled just to complete the set.

Every encounter can remain entirely grounded. Even all four together are **not
proof of supernatural involvement** and do not summon DARK actors, establish
their relationships or reveal another protected storyline.

## 5. Consequential behavior and audience response matrix

These describe witnessed choices and specific actor judgments, not permanent
classes or a global morality score. Characters retain different motives:
Constantine's pragmatism, Zatanna's protective choices, a guardian's ecological
priorities or Blood/Etrigan's competing intentions need not agree.

| Path / actual behavior | What attention requires | Possible distinct response | Persistent consequences / safeguards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Criminal: deliberate harm, coercion or misuse of entrusted material | A witness, bounded supernatural observation, attributable evidence or actual delivered report, plus an affected actor's motive/capacity | Investigate, warn, deny a particular service, protect a victim, contain a specific danger or intervene | Specific trust/access changes and source-backed consequences; no omniscient hero pursuit, guilt from faction/appearance or guaranteed fight |
| Reform: verified restitution, civilian protection, truthful correction and kept boundaries | Demonstrated acts reach that particular audience; credibility and conflicting prior evidence remain | Limited advice, introduction, safe accompaniment or protection from a specific threat; actor can decline | Earned trust/opportunity, not wiped history, rent relief, canceled court outcomes, automatic magic or party membership |
| Neutral / survival: leave, negotiate only accepted terms, protect privacy | Only the actual encounter/obligation is known; no hidden moral inference | Respect withdrawal, give ordinary safety information, defer contact or allow distance | Preserve grounded alternatives; no permanent neutral-class label, penalty for avoiding occult work or compulsory return |
| Refusal / ignored opportunity | Actual received invitation versus never-discovered event must be distinguished | Resolve independently; close a finite offer; avoid further unsolicited involvement | No invented promise, crime, betrayal, curse, debt or relationship reward from refusal/ignorance |
| Failure / partial result | Specific attempted actions and observed limits, not a generic “failed quest” label | Partial containment, delayed information, alternative credible help or a bounded loss tied to disclosed stakes | Explain source/cause, permit return to ordinary work/support; no irreversible hero/villain affiliation or soul ownership from survival |
| Betrayed / cheated / misled | A specific false claim, issuer and recipient; distinguish actor deception from player consent | Correct the record, seek another witness, exit a contract or preserve evidence | One party's belief need not update others; recovery does not erase the lie or make the victim guilty |
| Successful but unaffiliated | Actual bounded objective and settlement receipt | Thanks, a limited reference or no material reward, depending on informed actors | No automatic recruitment, universal approval or repeatable relationship/payment farming |

Refusal does not make a continuing harmful act consequence-free; protective
actors may still respond to **observed actual harm**. They do not punish a refusal
to join their organization or infer crimes from private history. A magical
observation must have an authored capability, target, time/order, content,
limitations and recipient; “psychic” is not a substitute for provenance.

## 6. Recovery, consent and ordinary-life softlock policy

- Every accepted episode needs a practical exit/withdrawal or bounded recovery
  path, including a zero-cash help route; availability must be checked before
  commitment. Recovery can be ordinary ethical care, a credible advocate, a safe
  public destination or revised terms. Treatment is not a guilt/morality flag.
- No permanent forced supernatural species, faction, curse, body control, eternal
  debt, required crime or lost ordinary ending from a routine failure. Specific
  exceptional irreversible story proposals would require separate approval.
- Player possession, coercion, distress and horror depiction need explicit future
  consent/content boundaries, summary/skip presentation and withdrawal. Skipping
  depiction must not surrender a gameplay choice or grant free outcomes.
- Do not manufacture a combat/health system here. If a proposed effect requires
  wounds, treatment, legal evidence, supervision or recovery state that does not
  exist, hold it until its separate approved adapter is available.
- Respect custody, restricted institutions, actual return routes and 18 TU/day;
  no teleport, unearned release, allowance reset or inaccessible mandatory errand.
  Keep three shared actions/day, finite deadlines and ordinary local alternatives.
- Assistance is a bounded authored relationship, not a repeatable money/home/job/
  law-enforcement solution. Do not gate an ethical provider behind a magical ally.

## 10. Manual paper scenario and design review

After an established grounded career, a lawful stage-service task yields an
unusual, documented discrepancy. The player can finish the ordinary task and
stop, help a coworker seek ordinary assistance, verify a received record, or
accept a later source-backed specialist referral. The source is not guaranteed
to be Zatanna personally. A misleading interpretation can lead to a correction
for one recipient; refusing further inquiry closes the offer without unpaid
occult debt or blocked work. A credible later harm report may attract a guardian,
while kept safety promises may earn limited support from an informed actor.

Meaningful decisions are disclosure, verification, protection, consent and exit.
Consequences are specific remembered terms/claims, future access and recipient
knowledge, not a global alignment score. Variety comes from credible conflicting
sources and different motives. Grind risks are repeat consultations, contrived
exposure and contact farming; finite causes/windows and exactly-once receipts
must block them. What remains shallow is authored scene variety and unapproved
power/recovery rules. **No new choice, event or persistent effect is implemented.**

the player has unlocked DARK progression**. This narrows G01's earlier optional

association. The [atlas finding](GOON_DARK_CANON_REGISTRY.md#actual-atlas-result--absent-no-replacement-or-new-id)
remains absent: no location, alias, route or place ID is created here.

### Knowledge, lasting aftermath and future validation

Keep **world truth** (incarnation, cause, presence, actual acts), **source
observations** (who saw what and when), **player knowledge** (received claims and
their limits) and **each NPC/institution's knowledge** distinct. Witness accounts
need not agree. Grundy's current memory/recognition is itself era/incarnation-
specific, not a copy of the player's journal. Neither a new incarnation nor a
correction retroactively teaches every witness or deletes preserved accounts.

Persist warranted injuries/recovery, witnessed conduct, relationship changes and
access decisions through their appropriate future authorities, with cause and
exactly-once receipts. No automatic criminal record from a frightening report;
ordinary evidence/institutional eligibility still applies. No magical healing,
erased rent/sentence, reset human mortality or inherited resurrection. Repeat
visits, alias changes, retry and save/reload cannot farm help, trust or rewards.

Before implementation, approve the incarnation/era, actual DARK unlock predicate,
original incident/lead and knowledge limits, noncombat risk/recovery, injury
authority if needed, and atlas/adoption contract if the swamp is added. Future
tests must cover the locked/unlocked-without-lead gates, independent occurrence,
absence/expiry, every matrix path, source ordering, rejected-command/RNG purity,
read-only inspection, exactly-once aftermath, rebirth without replayed rewards,
historical saves and unchanged budgets. These are **future tests, not executed
gameplay coverage**. No new event, scheduler, health system or combat is authorized.
