## Authority, fields and reading rules

The Task Force X addendum adds nine specialized references below (197 total
index records). [Its contract](GOON_TASK_FORCE_X_RECRUITMENT_DESIGN.md) owns new
Squad/government canon context and proposed screening, not a replacement criminal
organization registry. Existing Deadshot, Harley Quinn and Warren White identities
stay unchanged; their Squad-specific context is supplemental, not a second actor.

- Identity/key resolves an actor, distinct bound pair, linked persona, organization,
  category, mantle or vacant role. New index/runtime keys are documentation
  references only, never saved IDs or aliases replacing legacy keys.
- Aliases are limited edition-qualified lookup terms, not an exhaustive list.
  Unbound does not assert that a canonical identity does not exist.
- Continuity names a version/era or points to explicit source constraints.
- Affiliation/relationships marked **Ref** are pointer-valued: resolve that exact
  row/entry in **Authority** for verified, disputed and original ties. This avoids
  maintaining a second biography/alliance database. An unbound Goon pack does
  not assert that a DC character has no canon affiliations or relationships.
- Role/availability are proposals, not lore facts or encounter promises. Actual
  life/custody state, era, sources, commitments and consent still apply.
- Implementation distinguishes a bounded NPC/contact, static staff label, abstract
  faction, limited adapter, vacant role, design candidate and policy restriction.
  A name or place is not a fully simulated organization or staff personality.
- Authority owns details/confidence: minor identities in the minor registry,
  access in the encounter contract, organizations in the employer matrix,
  original crews in the ecosystem, civic roles in After Dark/institution contracts,
  and DARK evidence in its canon registry. Runtime definitions/code control
  implemented behavior. Overlapping entries share one identity with multiple
  authority pointers, never another biography.

This is **author-only**, never a player codex/search list, public UI fixture,
rumor feed or known-faction roster. Secret identities/protected metadata are
not player knowledge. No hero or magical observer reads private actions without
bounded provenance. No compulsory gang, boss, criminal or supernatural career.

## Preservation, exclusions and identity reconciliation

All 36 minor research entries, 36 overlapping encounter roles, 26 organization/
category records and original contacts are indexed without editing those records.
These are different sets. Terrible Trio members differ from the crew. Scarface
links to Arnold Wesker as a persona, not another payer. Jason Blood/Etrigan are
distinct bound wills, not an analogy for that persona. Swamp Thing, Robin, Batgirl
and Clayface require explicit actor/era selection; slots never duplicate people.

**Professor/Dr. Milo remains excluded**; no active row restores him. Crime Doctor,
Hugo Strange and an ethical-doctor slot are not his aliases. Ada Quinn, Ada Mercer
and Harley Quinn are distinct; Cal/Ada Mercer and Nia/Ruth Bell gain no family tie
from names. Civic Police/Night Watch/Detective Vale are not silently GCPD/Batman/
Gordon. GCPD Intake retains its bounded M019 mandate, not a renamed legacy faction.

Alternate holders mentioned in detailed sources are research context, not all
selected actors. Do not import every person in external sources, related-character
cards or metadata. Add detail first, then an index pointer. Vacant professional
roles are explicit gaps, not fabricated named DC personnel.
